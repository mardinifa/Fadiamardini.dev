Overview

Ulcer Health Risk Analysis is a data-driven health analytics project designed to explore patterns and factors associated with ulcer risk. The project transforms health-related data into meaningful insights through data preparation, analysis, and visualization, making complex health patterns easier to understand and communicate.

Key Features

  • Risk Factor Analysis: Examines health and lifestyle factors to identify patterns associated with ulcer risk
  • Data Preparation: Cleans, transforms, and structures health-related data to ensure consistency and analytical accuracy
  • Interactive Dashboard: Presents key risk indicators, demographic patterns, and analytical findings through clear visualizations
  • Pattern Analysis: Explores relationships between individual characteristics, lifestyle factors, and reported ulcer risk
  • Health Insights: Converts analytical findings into accessible insights that support health awareness and informed decision-making

Analysis Approach

The project follows a structured analytics workflow covering data cleaning, transformation, exploratory analysis, KPI development, and visualization. The prepared dataset is analyzed to identify meaningful patterns and relationships, which are then presented through an interactive dashboard.

Key Insights

The analysis provides a visual overview of ulcer-related risk patterns across different groups and contributing factors. By examining demographic and lifestyle characteristics alongside reported risk indicators, the dashboard makes it easier to identify notable patterns and communicate them clearly to stakeholders.

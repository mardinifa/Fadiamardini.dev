View on GitHub

Overview

U.S. Sales Performance & Profitability Insights is a business intelligence project designed to evaluate sales performance and profitability across products, categories, and regions. The analysis transforms transactional sales data into an interactive dashboard that highlights business performance, identifies key drivers, and supports data-driven decision-making.

Key Features

  • Sales Performance Analysis: Evaluates sales across products, categories, and geographic regions to identify major revenue contributors
  • Profitability Analysis: Examines profit performance to distinguish high-value products from areas requiring closer attention
  • Product & Category Analysis: Compares product and sub-category performance to identify stronger and weaker contributors
  • Regional Analysis: Analyzes geographic performance to highlight differences across U.S. regions
  • Interactive Dashboard: Presents KPIs, performance trends, and comparative insights through an intuitive business intelligence dashboard

Analysis Approach

The project follows a structured business intelligence workflow covering data preparation, transformation, KPI development, exploratory analysis, and dashboard design. Sales and profitability metrics are analyzed across multiple business dimensions to identify performance patterns and opportunities.

Key Insights

The analysis identified Technology as the leading product category, while Phones and Accessories emerged as strong contributors to profitability. At the sub-category level, Tables and Bookcases showed comparatively weaker performance. Regional analysis also highlighted the West as the strongest-performing region and the Central region as an area requiring closer performance evaluation.

Business Impact

The dashboard provides stakeholders with a consolidated view of sales and profitability performance, making it easier to identify revenue drivers, monitor weaker areas, and compare performance across regions and product categories.

Technologies

Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Excel, Data Analysis, Business Intelligence, Data Visualization.
